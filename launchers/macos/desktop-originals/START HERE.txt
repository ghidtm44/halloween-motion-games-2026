HALLOWEEN PLAYGROUND

Double-click Start Simple Playground.command for the original V1 experience:
people in three scenes, magic when waving, bats with hands up, candy for a small hop.

Click Open player, then Start camera when you are ready. Keep the Terminal window open.
The Simple control room is http://127.0.0.1:4318 while its launcher runs.

Double-click Start Corn Maze.command to open the preserved newer app.
Choose Corn Maze Quest in its control room. Its address is http://127.0.0.1:4317.

Stop the camera in one version before using the other. Stop a launcher with Control-C.
Keep the Simple Playground V1 folder with its launcher. The Corn Maze launcher points
to the existing project under Documents/Codex; that project has not been moved or changed.

Both versions have local vision assets and need no internet to play after setup.
